# Metalslime views

This file summarizes recent posts and replies from the `Metalslime` folder.
It is a time-slice framework, not a timeless truth.

## Core worldview

1. He looks at industry beta before stock alpha.
2. He treats the money-making effect as a leading market signal.
3. He dislikes pure storytelling without demand or earnings support.
4. He values first-hand tracking over recycled interpretation.
5. He respects circle of competence and avoids forced conclusions.

## Trading and position management

1. He cares about market position first, not just abstract value.
2. A rebound is often a risk-management window, not automatically a fresh bull case.
3. Mid-term positions can be built before the inflection is obvious in price.
4. If a style keeps losing in a broad bull phase, exposure may need to be cut.

## Sector views

### New energy

This is one of his strongest recent preferences.
He focuses on:

- demand growth, especially storage-related demand
- pricing power and pass-through
- profit transfer across the chain
- internal breadth and beta improvement
- whether a strong quarter is sustainable or only a short annualization illusion

He has also discussed sub-segments such as lithium, storage, batteries, LIFSI, liquid cooling, and material substitution such as copper replacing silver.

### Consumer

He is generally cautious.
His recurring idea is that many consumer sub-sectors no longer have strong industry beta and mostly rely on isolated stock alpha.
He also sees population structure and weak demand expectations as important for valuation pressure.

### Tires

This is a sector where he clearly built a mid-term thesis.
The main logic includes:

- better economics than many complete vehicle makers
- room for Chinese global share gains
- replacement-market economics
- brand-lag advantages
- high-barrier sub-segments such as giant tires and retread systems

He also stresses tariffs, global minimum tax, and raw-material inflation as real constraints.

### Games, AI, and pure-theme tech

He is cautious on games and many pure-theme tech trades.
He prefers earnings delivery over grand narrative and is skeptical of multi-year extrapolation from one strong quarter.

## One-line abstraction

`Use industry beta, money-making effect, real demand delivery, and position timing to pursue mid-term high-payoff directions, while staying skeptical of pure stories and linear extrapolation.`
